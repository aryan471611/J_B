The Hardest Part Was Leaving ❤️

Every meeting gave me memories. Every goodbye took a piece of my heart.

Looking at this photo, I don't just see pictures. I see train rides, long conversations, random laughs, warm hugs, shared meals and the memories we created together.

We never had the luxury of meeting every day, which is probably why every meeting felt so special. Every time we met, I counted the days until I could see you again.

It was always wonderful having you around me. Whether we were doing something special or simply spending time together, those moments always felt like the best part of my week.

The hardest part was never the journey to meet you. The hardest part was saying goodbye.

Every time I had to leave and return to my town, there was always a part of me that wished time would slow down just a little more. I never wanted those days to end.

What I miss most isn't just the meetings themselves. It's those final moments before leaving, the long hugs, the silence, and both of us not wanting the day to end.

Looking back now, I realize those moments meant so much because being with you always felt like where I wanted to be.

Something I Need To Say

This is probably the hardest part of everything I wanted to say.

I am genuinely sorry.

Sorry for every moment when I wasn't the partner you deserved. Sorry for the times I wasn't emotionally available. Sorry for the times I didn't listen carefully enough. Sorry for the times I didn't understand what you were trying to tell me.

Sorry for every expectation, every argument, every disagreement, every action and every word that hurt you, even when hurting you was never my intention.

Looking back today, I know I wasn't perfect. I know there are many things I could have handled better, many conversations I could have listened to more carefully, and many moments where I should have chosen understanding over being right.

I truly tried my best with the knowledge and maturity I had at that time. But sometimes trying your best and doing the right thing are not always the same.

Over the last two months, I have thought about us a lot. Maybe we are different in ways that neither of us fully understood. Maybe life became harder than we expected. Maybe some wounds became bigger than the love we were trying to protect.

I don't know when you will read this. Maybe today. Maybe weeks later. Or maybe never.

But I couldn't let this birthday pass without telling you how sorry I am.

You were a huge part of my life. You were there during some of my happiest memories. You gave me moments, experiences and lessons that I will carry with me forever.

Whether you forgive me or not is completely your choice. Whether we ever speak again is also your choice.

I am not writing this to force an outcome. I am writing this because after everything we shared, you deserved an honest apology from my heart.

If there is one thing I wish I could change, it would be every moment where my words, actions or decisions made you feel less loved, less understood or less important than you truly were.

For every hurt I caused, for every tear, for every disappointment, and for every moment where I failed to be the person you needed, I am deeply sorry.

No matter what happens next, I will always be grateful that our paths crossed.
